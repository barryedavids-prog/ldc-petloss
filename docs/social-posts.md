# Social media posts

Link to the **Squarespace page** that holds the check-in, not the GitHub Pages
address, so visitors land on the website with its menu and branding. Set that
page's social sharing image and description in Squarespace (Page settings >
Social Image / SEO), because that's what Facebook, Instagram and LinkedIn show in
the link preview. A calm photo of a pet, or of a lead or collar, works well.

Keep posts gentle and inviting. Say plainly that it's short and private.

---

**1. Reassurance**

> Losing a pet can hurt more than people expect, and it's not always easy to say
> so. I've put together a short, private check-in to help you notice how you've
> been feeling since your loss. Six gentle questions, about 2 minutes, and
> nothing is saved. [link]

**2. For people told to "move on"**

> "It was just a dog." "Isn't it time you got another cat?" If people around you
> haven't understood how much your pet meant, you're not alone. This short
> check-in is a quiet space to reflect, just for you. [link]

**3. Personal**

> When I lost my black lab Buddy, I learned how deep the grief for an animal can
> go. If you're missing a much-loved pet, this gentle 2-minute check-in might
> help you pause and take stock. [link]

**4. Short (for Instagram stories or X)**

> Missing a much-loved pet? A gentle, private 2-minute check-in. [link]
